Timothy Treadwell spent thirteen summers living among the brown bears of Katmai National Park in Alaska, filming them and giving them names. His story became the 2005 documentary *Grizzly Man* by Werner Herzog.

## Living among the bears

Treadwell camped for long stretches in bear country, including an area of dense brush known as the Grizzly Maze. Park rangers repeatedly cited him for breaking park rules, but he kept returning every summer.

## A later stay than usual

In 2003, his thirteenth season, he stayed in the park later in the year than he usually did.

## October 2003

That October, Treadwell and his girlfriend, Amie Huguenard, were attacked and killed by a bear at their camp. What led up to the attack is unknown.

## The tape

A video camera at the camp had been switched on during the attack, but the lens cap was still on. It captured no images, only a few minutes of sound. The recording has never been publicly released.

## The takeaway

Thirteen summers with grizzly bears, ending on a tape nobody plays.
