Watch a frog eat and you may see it blink hard as it swallows. It is not blinking. It is using its eyeballs to push the food down.

## Eyes that sink into the mouth

Each time a frog swallows, both eyes are pulled down and retract deep into the mouth cavity. Only thin tissue separates the eyes from the inside of the mouth.

## What the X-rays showed

Researchers filmed northern leopard frogs eating with X-ray video. The footage showed the retracted eyes reaching the prey and pressing it back toward the oesophagus.

## The nerve experiment

To test whether the eyes really help, the researchers cut the nerves to the muscles that pull the eyes down. The frogs could still swallow, but they needed about 74% more swallows per meal — almost twice as many.

## The takeaway

A frog blinking mid-meal is swallowing with its eyeballs.
