In September 2004, a tourist on safari in South Africa's Kruger National Park filmed one of the most famous wildlife encounters ever recorded. It became known as the Battle at Kruger.

## The lions strike

At Transport Dam, lions charged a herd of African buffalo and separated a calf. The calf and a lion fell into the water together.

## Then the crocodiles

In the water, two Nile crocodiles grabbed the same calf. For a moment it was a tug of war between the lions on the bank and the crocodiles below. The crocodiles gave up, and the lions dragged the calf onto land.

## The herd comes back

The buffalo herd returned. One buffalo charged and flicked a lion into the air, and the herd pressed in until the lions were driven off.

## The calf walks away

According to the most detailed accounts, the calf, still alive, got up and rejoined the herd. The video was uploaded to YouTube in 2007, went viral, and was later used in a National Geographic documentary.

## The takeaway

Lions, then crocodiles, and the buffalo calf still walked away alive.
