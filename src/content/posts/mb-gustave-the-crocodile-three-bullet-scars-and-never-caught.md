In Burundi, along the Ruzizi River and the northern shore of Lake Tanganyika, lived a huge male Nile crocodile that locals named Gustave. He was seen with three bullet-wound scars, and no one ever managed to catch him.

## A trap built for one crocodile

In the early 2000s, naturalist Patrice Faye and a documentary team set out to capture him alive. They built a trap cage weighing about 2,000 pounds and nearly 30 feet long, with a hidden infrared camera inside.

## Every bait failed

For weeks, the team tried different baits. Gustave did not take any of them.

## The goat and the storm

In their final week, before conflict forced the team to leave the country, they tied a live goat inside the cage. That night a thunderstorm knocked out the camera. By morning the cage was partly underwater, and the goat was gone. With the camera dead, nobody can say what took it.

## The legend

Some stories claim Gustave killed up to 300 people. That number is poorly sourced; other estimates put the toll at around 60 or fewer. His exact length and weight are also unknown.

## The takeaway

Three bullets, one giant trap, and Gustave was never caught.
