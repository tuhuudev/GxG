The popular picture is of hyenas sneaking in to steal from lions. In Tanzania's Ngorongoro Crater, long-term research says it usually goes the other way.

## A crater full of hyenas

Up to 550 spotted hyenas live in the Ngorongoro Crater, alongside roughly 25 to 40 adult lions. That many hyenas could not possibly live on lion leftovers, and the Ngorongoro Hyena Project reports that they hunt most of their own food.

## Size against numbers

A male lion is about twice the size of a spotted hyena and three to four times as heavy. Hyenas cannot win on size, so they win, when they win, on numbers.

## Six to one

According to the Hyena Project, hyenas can keep or take over a kill from lionesses and young males only when they recruit at least six times more animals than the lions. With fewer, the lions take the carcass. In the crater, lions steal more kills from hyenas than hyenas steal from lions.

## No crowd beats an adult male

When adult male lions arrive, they dominate or take over the kill no matter how many hyenas gather. The hyenas may harass them, but usually they wait nearby until the lions leave. Lions are also the main cause of death for hyenas in the crater.

## The takeaway

Five hundred and fifty hyenas, and they still wait for one male lion to walk away.
