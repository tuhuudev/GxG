The bald eagle is America's national bird. One of its founding fathers did not think much of it.

## Franklin's letter

On January 26, 1784, Benjamin Franklin wrote to his daughter Sarah Bache that the eagle "is a Bird of bad moral Character." His complaint: the eagle perches "on some dead Tree near the River", and when the fishing hawk "has at length taken a Fish", the eagle "pursues him and takes it from him." The fishing hawk he meant is the osprey.

## How the osprey fishes

According to the US National Park Service, ospreys soar high above the water and then dive feet first at full force, often fully submerging to bring up their catch. The osprey is the only raptor whose outer toe is reversible, so it can hold a fish with two toes in front and two behind, and barbed pads on its feet help it grip slippery prey. It carries the fish head forward, the most aerodynamic way to fly with it.

## The chase

That loaded climb away from the water is when an eagle may take off after it. Observers have photographed bald eagles chasing ospreys that carry fish. Sometimes the osprey lets go and the eagle grabs the fish; sometimes the osprey keeps it and the eagle gives up.

## The takeaway

More than two hundred years after Franklin's letter, his "bird of bad moral character" still eats an osprey's catch now and then.
