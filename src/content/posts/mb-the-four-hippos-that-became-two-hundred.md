In 2026, Colombia approved a plan to cull up to 80 wild hippos. Every one of those animals descends from just four.

## Four hippos for a private zoo

The drug lord Pablo Escobar smuggled hippos into Colombia for the private zoo on his estate. Most of the zoo's animals were moved after his death, but the hippos were left behind.

## After 1993, they walked out

Escobar was killed in 1993. With nobody controlling them, the hippos left the estate and spread into the rivers nearby. Colombia has no natural predators for them, and the population kept growing.

## From four to about two hundred

Recent counts put the herd at about two hundred animals, twice as many as earlier estimates. Nobody managed to stop the growth.

## Why it is so hard to stop

Sterilization was tried, but it is slow and expensive: sterilizing a single hippo costs almost ten thousand dollars. Officials even planned to ship some of the animals abroad, to India and Mexico.

## What happens next

Without action, scientists project the population could reach about a thousand by 2035. That is why the government has now turned to culling.

## The takeaway

Four hippos became two hundred, and the next step could be a thousand.
